## Per-claim notes (only where not plainly confirmed)

- **C1:** Use arXiv 2606.13108 as the PP-OCRv6 citation and 2603.24373 (CVPR 2026) as the PP-OCRv5 paper. The PaddleOCR 3.0 tech report (2507.05595) remains a separate citation. The release-date part of the "no paper" note (v3.7.0 on 2026-06-11) is correct.
- **C2:** "RepLKFPN neck" is the paper's name. The medium config actually instantiates `RepLKPAN`. The paper says "LCNetV4"; the code and docs say "PPLCNetV4". All numbers match.
- **C6:** Change "+0.6–1.0 on SVTR" to "+0.5–1.1 across SVTR-T/S/B/L".
- **C7:** The +11.5 / +5.8 gains are on real labelled data (ARD). With synthetic training data the gap shrinks to +6.6 / +4.4.
- **C8:** "Consistency" is wrong. Mean Teacher hurt CRNN (−0.2). Pseudo-Label gave +2.8 and PL+RotNet gave +3.4, both on top of augmentation.
- **C9:** Teacher task adaptation is +0.8 AP. The B-vs-L teacher numbers are student AP under ECDet-M.
- **C10:** Cosine = 53.7 AP (vs MSE 52.7, baseline 52.5).
- **C11b:** DINOv3 License does not prohibit commercial use. It is not OSI-open (use restrictions, termination, attribution). Have legal review it before shipping distilled weights.
- **C12:** PaddleOCR DBLoss is Dice-based on the shrink map by default and in shipped configs, not BCE.
- **C14:** The comparison is PAN pixel aggregation vs GPU max-pool dilation, not polygon unclip. FAST has no venue beyond arXiv.
